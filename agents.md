# Image Style Guidelines

All generated or hand-drawn pictures used in this project (such as for HGL/z) must conform to the following artistic style:

- **Style**: Monochromatic graphite pencil sketch on warm off-white textured paper.
- **Details**: Featuring clean hand-drawn line art, detailed cross-hatching, parallel line shading, and subtle smudging for depth.
- **Background**: Rendered with a soft graphite halo vignette behind the subjects against a plain paper background.

# Article Header Images

Every article has a header image in this style:

- Each post is a page bundle at `content/posts/<slug>/index.md`, and its image lives in the same directory (e.g. `content/posts/hglz/hglz.jpeg`).
- The image is the first thing in the article body, placed immediately after the front matter, with the article title as alt text:

  ```markdown
  ---
  title: "HGL/z"
  ...
  ---
  ![HGL/z](hglz.jpeg)

  First paragraph...
  ```

- When creating a new article, add a matching image in this style: monochromatic graphite pencil sketch on warm off-white textured paper, featuring clean hand-drawn line art, detailed cross-hatching, parallel line shading, and subtle smudging for depth, rendered with a soft graphite halo vignette behind the subjects against a plain paper background.
